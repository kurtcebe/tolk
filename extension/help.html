Tolk help

Tolk is a reader for the language you're learning. It reads the text aloud and highlights each word as it's spoken, so you can follow along at a speed that suits you.

Tolk is still being built. For now, clicking its toolbar icon opens a sample text in Dutch.

Book and Learner

Both modes show the text in exactly the same place, so you can switch at any time, at the top right, without losing your line.

  • Learner keeps the player on screen.
  • Book hides the player for quiet reading. The speaker button at the top right brings it back, without starting to speak. Switching to Book pauses any speech.

Reading along

Press Play to start. A band marks the current sentence, and a stronger colour marks the current word.

  • Jump to a word: once reading has started, click any word to continue from there. This also works while paused.
  • Select text: dragging across text or double-clicking a word selects it, without moving your place.
  • Move by sentence: use the previous and next buttons.
  • Scrolling: the text holds still while the highlight moves down the page, then scrolls once to bring it back up. If you scroll yourself, the page stops following and the voice carries on. Choose Back to reading position to return.

Speed

The mark in the middle of the slider is the voice's normal speed. Tolk doesn't show percentages because each voice responds differently, so normal is the only speed it can promise. Reset takes you back there.

Some voices change speed in steps, so a small move may sound the same. How slow a voice can go also differs from one voice to the next, and trying another voice may get you a slower speed.

Voices

The speaker button in the player lists the voices for the language of the text. There are two kinds:

  • Local voices come with your computer and run on it. Tolk uses one by default.
  • Remote voices come with Chrome and send the text to Google to be spoken. Tolk only uses one if you pick it, and the player tells you while it's selected.

Your choice is remembered on this computer. If you use Chrome on another computer, you choose again there.

If there's no voice for your language

Tolk can't download voices itself. Add one in your computer's own settings, under speech, spoken content or language. The exact place depends on your system. You may need to restart Chrome before the new voice shows up in Tolk.

If only the sentence is highlighted

Not every voice reports which word it's on. Tolk finds this out when a voice first speaks, and then highlights the sentence alone rather than guess a word. A remote voice usually works this way. A local voice usually highlights each word, so try one if you want word highlighting.

Keyboard shortcuts

These work while the player is visible, except when you're in a control that uses the same key, such as the speed slider.

Key What it does
Space Play or pause
← → Previous or next sentence
[ ] Slower or faster
Esc Close the voice list or settings

Privacy

  • Tolk has no account, analytics or crash reports.
  • Tolk doesn't send your reading anywhere itself.
  • The one exception is a remote voice, if you pick one: Chrome sends the text to Google to be spoken.
  • Your settings, such as colours and speed, follow your Chrome profile, so Chrome stores them in your Google account. Your voice choice stays on this computer.
