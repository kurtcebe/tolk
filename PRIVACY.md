# Tolk privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026.

Tolk is a Chrome extension for reading web articles and EPUB books in a language you're learning. It has no server, account, analytics, telemetry or crash reports. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What Tolk handles, and where it stays

- **The page you open in Tolk.** Tolk reads a page only when you click its toolbar icon or press its shortcut, and only that page. The article's text is kept in the browser's memory until you close its tab or quit Chrome. It's never saved to disk or to your library.
- **Books you import.** EPUB files you import, and your place in them, are stored in this browser on this computer. Opening a book loads nothing from the internet, even if the book refers to something online.
- **Saved words.** The words you save, with their translations, the sentence they came from and a link to their source, are stored in this browser on this computer.
- **Reading history.** Daily totals of reading time and words read per language, which books you've finished, and the colours and shapes the library uses to draw them. Tolk never records what you read or which pages you opened. *Clear reading history* in the library deletes it.
- **Your voice choice** is stored on this computer.

Uninstalling Tolk deletes everything in this list. Exporting saved words is the way to keep them.

## Language detection and translation

Tolk detects the language of the text and translates it with Chrome's built-in on-device AI. Both run on your computer, and the text isn't sent anywhere.

The first time a language needs it, Chrome downloads a language pack or detection model from Google. That only happens after you click a Download or Translate button. Your reading isn't sent with the download.

## What leaves your computer

Two things can, and both are sent by Chrome rather than by Tolk:

- **Settings.** Tolk keeps its settings in Chrome's synced storage, so if Chrome sync is on, Chrome stores them in your Google account. They are the colour scheme, font, text size, line spacing, Book or Learner mode, your own language, and a reading speed for each language you've listened to. Your reading, books, saved words and history are never synced.
- **Text read by a remote voice.** Chrome offers some voices that run on Google's servers. If you pick one, Chrome sends the text being spoken to Google. Tolk uses a voice on your computer by default, uses a remote one only if you choose it, and says so in the player while it's selected.

Google's handling of these is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What Tolk doesn't do

- It doesn't make network requests of its own, or load code or content from the internet.
- It doesn't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and it doesn't use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than the reader's own features.
- The use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date, and the history is in this repository.

Questions go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/kurtcebe/tolk/issues).
